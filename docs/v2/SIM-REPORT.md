## 5. Spec gaps (revision 4)

1. **D12(b) per-level mean with censoring:** D12 does not say how to average a level some learners never finish. Pairs are included when both learners reached the level; a censored level (60-session cap) counts as 60. The p90 uses the same pairs. Stuck>20 and KS use all learners (∞ as a tie).
2. **D10 score for mastered facts:** DECISIONS says a mastered fact shows a distinct state instead of marks, but defines the event sum as marks + 3×mastered with marks(f) = min(3, cW) for every non-new, non-struggling fact. Implemented literally: a mastered fact contributes min(3, cW) + 3. (With "mastered contributes only 3", mastering a fact that already had 3 marks would not count as advancement.)
3. **Record eligibility** uses all draw answers (counted or not), as D3 says "answers with source draw"; the evidence buffer uses counted draws only (D2).
4. **Visible "moved up a status"** (experience metric, not a rule): struggling→learning, learning→mastered, new→mastered; new→learning does not count. Badges modelled as in revision 3 (First Run, Hot/On Fire in-session streaks, Perfect, Comeback, Record Breaker, 3-Day).
5. **Fast-track "at least one of those sessions not inferred"** is checked as "some correct counted attempt is live"; the sim has no inferred attempts, so this never binds.
6. Revision 3 interpretations that still apply: floor not applied at question 1; floor restricts the chosen pool then the other pool; over-cap carried queue items wait; L1 intro running cap; queue items carry to the next session with their remaining delay; R4/L9 fail when the evidence is shorter than the window.

## 6. Model limitations

- Same learner models as revision 3 (independent Bernoulli answers, fixed latency ranges, 1 session/day, always the progress level, random 70/30 mode mix, assumed learning/forgetting parameters). Learning-learner numbers are directional.
- Marks are measured over the current level's table facts only, as D10 defines; the kid may also notice review facts moving, which the metric ignores.
- Placement, drop-down, XP-per-minute and inference (D5, D5b, D6 farming check) were not re-run for revision 4; the archived revision 3 results for them are unaffected by the rev-4 changes except through faster Practice progression. `placement.ts` and `xp.ts` remain as reference implementations.

### 1. Summary

- **Speed (P4 / E):** latency never affects status, completion or record eligibility — exact replay at ×0.3 and ×3 latency is identical for all three slowness modes (§3). Statistically, rev-3 slowness (on unmastered facts) and the naive option 2 both fail E(b) (KS p ≤ 0.008; naive makes slow children ~2 sessions slower). **Option 2 budget-neutral passes Quick and Mixed but fails one Practice cell by the strict rule (L5 median 3 vs 4; P(≤3) 51.1% vs 49.1% at 4 000 learners each, KS p = 0.94, mean 39.1 vs 39.1)**. That is a median sitting on an integer boundary, not a shift, but the run exits 1 as instructed. Basis: static fast vs slow p = 0.90.
- **Provisional mastery → off** (static and learning). It almost never fires: 0.2–1.6% of gating facts at level-up, ≤ 0.09 of 9 gating facts after the first L1 session. Its confirm items slow Practice play substantially: learning-typical 82 → 66 sessions to finish L9 when off, learning-slow academic stuck 71% → 29%, static 0.80 139 → 93.5. Only benefit: slightly fewer experience droughts in Practice/Mixed (learning typical 11% vs 17%) (§7 B, P1).
- **P1 verdict:** provisional does *not* let 75–80% learners advance too easily. It makes them slower. 20–29% of the few provisional facts at level-up are revoked on the next attempt (6–11% for p = 0.90).
- **Structural bug in rule (b) (static + learning, Practice/Rush):** "correct attempts in W span ≥ 2 sessions" is erased when one session gives a fact ≥ 4 counted attempts (confirms, carried reinforcement). All-Rush players below p ≈ 1.0 can never master anything. Candidate fix V1 (check the 2-session span over all correct counted attempts) cuts learning-typical Practice from 82 to 62 sessions and makes Rush playable (§7 V, V-Rush).
- **Mastery window:** keep 4 / 3-of-4 / fast-track 2 / 2 sessions. masteredMinCorrect 2 is only ~8% faster, makes 50% accuracy count as mastered, and raises experience-stuck (learning typical 80% → 98%). minDistinctSessions 1 changes nothing in Quick (static + learning, §7 H).
- **R4 (P2):** keep 0.85 / 20. Near the threshold, R4 is the sole blocker in 25–56% of level runs and bounces in 16–43%. It adds ~7–29 sessions over a journey (static 0.80/0.85, learning slow/start75). Window 30 is worse everywhere: slower and more stuck. 0.80 saves ~5–9 sessions, 0.90 costs ~14–17 (§7 P2, H).
- **Mixed (L9) is the grind point under rev-3 (B@0.88)** for near-threshold learners: learning slow/start75 need 25–30 L9 sessions in Quick (2.1–2.5× a typical level, 61–65% academic-stuck). Static 0.80 needs 25 (×3.3). It is not a grind for p ≥ 0.90 static or learning-fast (P3, §6).
- **A ≈ B:** the "≤ 2 struggling facts" clause changes L9 stuck rates by ≤ 1 pp for every learning learner (≤ 7 pp for static 0.75 in Practice). **C as written has a frozen-measure trap:** once every prior-mastered fact has appeared, its first-appearance accuracy can never change, so 28–63% of learning learners are permanently stuck at 0.85 (7–28% at 0.80; 0% for the rev-3 improving model). **Cr** (C with a rolling 20-answer retention check, a sim-only candidate) gives L9 in 2–8 sessions (median; ×0.2–0.7 of a typical level), ≤ 4% academic-stuck (>20) and 4–12% experience-stuck within L9 for every learning learner.
- **L9 threshold:** 0.85 and 0.88 behave alike only for p ≥ 0.90. For learning learners near the edge 0.88 costs +1–10 median sessions and roughly doubles stuck (learning start75 mixed: 10 → 14 sessions, 16% → 33%). 0.90 is clearly harsher. Recommend **0.85** (= levelAccuracyMin, one number).
- **L9 window bug:** with a 50-answer window and "≥ 3 sessions", all-Practice error-free players (25 draw answers per session) and all Rush players can never finish L9. Static 1.00 in Practice is 100% stuck. Fixes: V3 (min sessions 2, fixes Practice only) or V4 (window extends back to span 3 sessions, fixes both). Cr avoids it (§7 V).
- **Experience stuck (new metric):** all-Quick play produces long droughts. 66–91% of learning learners have ≥ 3 sessions in a row with no visible progress, versus 9–17% in Practice. No RULES value fixes this (H sweeps: 76–99%). It needs a visible per-session progress signal, which is a UX decision outside RULES.
- **Learning learners** progress at ~6–16 sessions per level (typical: 90 sessions to finish L9 in Quick, 82 in Practice). Weak clusters recover slowly: the learning-clustered cluster reaches mean p 0.51 and 19–33% mastered by session 60. Cluster facts are first mastered at sessions ~50–80, mostly after the child has moved past L6–L8 on the allowance (§4).
- **Placement:** 3.6 questions for a beginner, 7–11 otherwise, cap hit ≤ 5.4%. Exact start 60–95%; over-placed by 1+ level 21–39% when unknown facts are guessed at 30%. The 70% drop-down offer does not discriminate: it fires for 59–100% of correctly placed children at L1–L3 and catches ≤ 15% of over-placed children at L6+ (static, §7 F).
- **Success floor 0.40:** met in ~100% of enforceable sessions; the natural likely-correct share is ~62–64%, so 0.3/0.5 barely matter. **XP:** progress-level XP/min is ≥ 3× steady replay and above one-time first-finish replay for every learner and mode tested. No farming case.
- **Static low-p learners** (0.60–0.75) never certify L9 and often not L1. That is expected for a permanently 60–75% child and not a defect by itself. Static 0.80 finishes L1–L8 but 41% stall at L9 in Quick.

### 8. Spec gaps

1. **Branch base.** The worktree branch was created from main (9b0121e), not v2/integration. I fast-forwarded it to v2/integration (28c4889) to get the spec. The system node is v24.20; Node 26.10 (fnm) was used. The code uses only erasable syntax.
2. **Success floor at the first question:** likelyDrawn / questionsSoFar is undefined at q = 0, so the floor is not applied to question 1.
3. **"≥ 2 counted attempts with ≥ 75% correct" (likely-correct)** is measured over W (last 4 counted), not all of C.
4. **Floor with two pools:** restrict the chosen pool to likely candidates; if it has none, use the other pool's likely candidates. Carried-hard facts over the cap and capped intro facts are excluded. A due non-likely queue item waits at most 3 questions, then is served.
5. **Carried-hard cap:** an over-cap carried-hard queue item waits (it stays queued and carries to the next session). A pool draw that lands on a carried-hard fact over the cap is redrawn from the level pool (literal "draw from level pool instead").
6. **L1 intro cap** is a running prefix cap: an intro fact may be drawn from the pool only if (introDrawn + 1) ≤ 0.20·(q + 1). Queue items for intro facts are not blocked, but they count.
7. **Later-check** is scheduled only after a *correct reintroduce*. The existing orchestrator re-schedules after every correct later-check, which would chain forever across sessions.
8. **Confirms (step 7):** scheduled after any correct counted attempt while the fact has < 2 confirms this session and none pending, only if due < session length. Mastered facts get confirms too (literal). Confirms never carry to the next session. Reintroduce/later-check items carry with their remaining relative delay (existing behaviour) and count in the new session.
9. **Level answer buffer** stores only draw/confirm answers, so R4/L9 windows are full-size. R4 with < 20 answers fails; L9 with < 50 answers fails.
10. **Provisional gap rule** is applied to *all* consecutive counted attempts in the session, so an ordinary redraw within 8 questions blocks provisional. This is the main reason provisional almost never fires.
11. **Rule (b) span over W** (literal) is erased when a session gives a fact ≥ 4 counted attempts. This is the Practice/Rush pathology; V1 is the tested alternative.
12. **L9 "≥ 3 sessions" inside a 50-answer window** is literal, so Practice error-free and all Rush players can never complete L9 (V3/V4 tested).
13. **Placement cap:** "lowest level not yet passed" treats levels jumped below a passed probe as passed, so start = highest passed probe + 1. The tiebreaker is a random gating fact other than the two probed. PLAN T6's "stops on 2 consecutive misses" is not in DECISIONS and was not implemented.
14. **XP:** per-correct counts every correct answer (reinforcement included). The completed-level rate is ⌊correct/5⌋ per session. With one session per day, the record daily cap never binds.
15. **Visible-progress events** compare session-start vs session-end status. new→learning does not count; new→provisional/mastered does. Badges modelled: First Run, Hot Streak / On Fire (in-session streaks), Perfect, Comeback Kid, Record Breaker, 3-Day. Level Mastered = level-up; Speedster is unreachable (no replays). Droughts are counted until the official L9 finish.
16. **Learning model:** a fact's first-ever counted correct attempt gives no gain (it has no previous counted attempt). The reinforcement gain applies at most once per fact per session and not on top of the new-session gain. Forgetting applies at session end to facts not seen that session (1 session = 1 day), decaying toward p0. For "start ~75%", every p0 is shifted by one constant so the mean over facts *owned* by L1–L4 = 0.75 (clamped to 0.05–0.97).
17. **Capstone (C):** "previously mastered" = status mastered at L9 entry; "carried" = everything else (including provisional/new). The selection split (50% of pool draws to still-unmastered carried facts, the rest to prior-mastered facts, unseen first) is my parameter; the brief gives none. A first appearance counts any source. ≥ 20 first appearances is strict. Cr (rolling retention) is my candidate, not the brief's.
18. **Option 2 two-stage:** stage 2 re-picks among the mastered candidates of the same pool after the last-3 exclusion, weighted by slowness only, on its own RNG stream.
19. **Static clustered-weak:** non-cluster facts are at 0.90 (not renormalised so the overall mean is 0.90). **Rev-3 "improving":** "different session than the previous one" = the session of the previous rise.
20. **Spec-D "hard"** includes status learning, so every fact with a single counted attempt counts as hard. The p < 0.7 view is reported separately (A4).

### 9. Model limitations

- Answers are independent Bernoulli draws: no fatigue, streak effects, typos, or within-session learning (except the learning models' explicit gains). Latency is independent of correctness.
- Exactly one session per day, no rest days or holidays, and the child always plays the progress level. Mode choice is random (70/30), not driven by motivation. There is no drop-out model, so "experience stuck" is only a proxy for frustration.
- Learning-model parameters (p0 by product, η, 3%/day forgetting toward p0, 0.97 cap) are assumptions, not fitted to Mikaela's data. Results for learning learners are directional.
- Placement assumes unknown facts are guessed at a flat 30% and known facts at 95%. Real children partially know neighbouring tables (e.g. ×10 is easy), so over-placement and drop-down behaviour are rough. The drop-down check starts from a fresh state (placement attempts and placementLikely are not carried).
- D5b start-level inference for existing v1 profiles was not simulated.
- XP/min uses a fixed 1.5 s non-answer overhead. Record XP and badges are a subset of D6, and replay sessions do not award records.
- The experience-stuck metric counts only the listed events. Other UI signals (XP bar movement, per-session "facts got stronger") would change it.
- Rush was only simulated for a few learners (100 per cell, 20-session cap), and it hits the rule-(b) pathology hard.
- E(b) uses integer session counts. When the true median sits on an integer boundary, |Δmedian| ≤ 0.5 can fail on noise-sized differences even at 4 000 learners per side.
